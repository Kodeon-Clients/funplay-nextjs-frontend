A warranty is a promise about how long parts of the equipment should last if it is installed and looked after properly. An annual maintenance contract (AMC) is a service that helps it get there. Both are worth understanding before you sign a purchase order, because the terms differ a lot between suppliers.

## How playground warranties work

Playground warranties are almost always given **component by component**, because different parts wear at very different rates:

<Table caption="How warranty periods usually differ by component" head={["Component","Typical cover","Why"]} rows={[["Galvanised steel frames and posts","Longest","Structural parts, protected against rust"],["Roto-moulded plastic parts","Medium","Can fade or crack with long UV exposure"],["Bearings, hangers, chains, springs","Shortest","Moving parts that wear with every use"],["Paint and colour","Often limited or excluded","Some fading in strong sun is normal"],["Safety surfacing","Separate terms","Depends on the product and the base it is laid on"]]} />

As an example, our standard terms are 3 years on galvanised pipes and 1 year on bearings, and we confirm the terms for each item with the quote. Whatever supplier you choose, get the terms in writing for each component.

## Read the exclusions

Most disputes come from the exclusions, not the warranty period. Typical exclusions:

- Vandalism, misuse and accidental damage.
- Damage from floods, storms, fire or falling trees.
- Normal wear of moving parts after their warranty period.
- Colour fading.
- Problems caused by poor maintenance, or by repairs, changes or relocation done by someone else.
- Installation by a third party not approved by the manufacturer.

## What an AMC should include

An AMC is a service agreement for regular inspection and maintenance. A useful one includes:

<Checklist title="What a good playground AMC covers" items={["A fixed number of visits per year (quarterly is common for busy sites)","Inspection of every unit, the surfacing and the fixings, against a written checklist","Tightening, lubrication and minor adjustments during the visit","Replacement of small wear parts, or clear rates for them","A written report after each visit with photos and recommended actions","A response time for urgent safety issues between visits","An annual main inspection"]} />

## Do you need an AMC?

An AMC is especially useful when:

- Nobody on site is trained or available to do monthly hands-on checks.
- The play area is heavily used, as in schools and busy parks.
- The site has an outdoor gym, merry-go-rounds or other equipment with many moving parts.
- The committee or management changes often and records get lost.

If you have a capable estate team, you may only need the supplier's annual inspection plus spares on call. Our [playground safety inspection checklist](/blog/playground-safety-inspection-checklist.html) gives your team a routine to follow.

## Questions to ask before you buy

1. What is the warranty period for each component, in writing?
2. What exactly is excluded?
3. How do we make a claim, and how quickly do you respond?
4. Are spare parts available, and for how long after purchase?
5. Do you offer an AMC? What does it cover and how often do you visit?
6. Will you train our maintenance staff at handover?
