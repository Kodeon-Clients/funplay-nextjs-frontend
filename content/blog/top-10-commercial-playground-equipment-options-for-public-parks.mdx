A public park playground is the hardest-working play area of all. It is open to everyone, all day, often without supervision, in full sun and monsoon rain. Equipment that suits a school or a society may not survive a park. Here are ten types of commercial equipment that work well in Indian parks, and why.

## 1. Large multi play stations

A big station with several towers, slides, climbers and bridges serves many children at once and becomes the landmark of the park. Choose one sized for the crowds you expect on a Sunday evening. See [what is a multi play station](/blog/what-is-a-multi-play-station.html).

## 2. Swing sets with nest swings

Swings are always the busiest item. Add at least one nest swing: several children can share it, and children with limited mobility can use it lying down.

## 3. Merry-go-rounds and spinners

A big draw for all ages. Choose robust designs with sealed bearings and a level surface around the edge.

## 4. Climbing nets and rotating climbers

Tepee nets, dome climbers and rotating net towers give older children the challenge they look for and many can climb at once.

## 5. See-saws

Simple, sturdy and social. Multi-seat versions serve more children.

## 6. Spring riders

Cheap, durable and perfect for toddlers. Place a group of them in the toddler zone.

## 7. Balance trails

Beams, stepping posts and wobbly bridges at ground level. They spread children out and suit all ages.

## 8. Inclusive and ground-level play

Play panels, sensory elements, wide ramps and accessible swings. A public park should welcome every child. See [the growing need for inclusive playgrounds](/blog/growing-need-for-inclusive-playgrounds-in-indian-communities.html).

## 9. Nature and themed play

Structures inspired by trees, animals and Indian games make a park memorable and fit green settings. See [using natural elements with play equipment](/blog/using-natural-elements-with-play-equipment-for-immersive-spaces.html).

## 10. Outdoor gym equipment

Walkers, cross trainers, twisters and pull-up stations bring adults and older people into the park. See [how to plan an outdoor gym in a park](/blog/how-to-plan-an-outdoor-gym-in-a-park.html).
