Good equipment in a bad layout is still a dangerous playground. Most collisions and many falls happen because of where things are, not what they are: a swing next to a path, a slide that ends at a bench, two climbers crowded together. Planning the layout well costs nothing extra and prevents a lot of injuries.

## Safety zones

Every piece of equipment needs a **safety zone** (also called the impact area or safe play area): the space around it where a child might fall, jump off or move while using it. That zone must be:

- clear of other equipment, benches, walls, trees and bins,
- clear of paths and through-routes, and
- covered with an impact-absorbing surface.

Under EN 1176 the zone generally extends at least 1.5 m around most equipment and grows with the fall height. Our product pages list the **safe play area** for each item, for example a play tower that needs about 24 by 23 ft for the product needs about 30 by 29 ft including its safe area.

## A step-by-step layout method

1. **Measure the site** and mark fixed features: trees, walls, drains, manholes, entrances, shade at different times of day.
2. **List the equipment** with the safe area of each item.
3. **Place the largest structure first,** usually the multi play station, near the centre or as the focal point.
4. **Put swings at the edges,** with their long zones parallel to the boundary.
5. **Group toddler equipment** in one corner, away from swings and the big structure.
6. **Draw the routes** children will run between items and the entrance. Keep them out of swing zones and slide exits.
7. **Check sight lines** from where supervisors will stand or sit.
8. **Add seating, shade, bins and signs** outside the safety zones.
9. **Draw the surfacing** across every safety zone, thicker under higher equipment.

## Common layout mistakes

- Swings in the middle of the play area.
- Slide exits facing walls, fences or benches.
- Paths cutting through safety zones.
- Toddler equipment next to fast spinning or climbing equipment.
- Tall structures blocking the view of a corner.
- Benches placed inside safety zones after installation.
