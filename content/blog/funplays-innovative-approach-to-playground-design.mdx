Since 2002, we have designed and built playgrounds for schools, housing societies, builders, parks, resorts and government bodies across India. A few ideas guide everything we design.

## Design for the site

No two sites are the same. A narrow podium in Mumbai, a school courtyard in Telangana and a resort lawn in Tamil Nadu need different solutions. We start with the space, the users and the setting, and adapt layouts, heights and colours to fit.

## High play value

A good structure gives many children many ways to play at once: climbing, sliding, balancing, hiding, pretending. Play value per child matters more than the number of items.

## Play and learning together

Counting frames, shape boards, music elements and story-led themes turn play equipment into a learning environment, especially for younger children. See [how play-based learning can transform education](/blog/how-play-based-learning-can-transform-education-in-india.html).

## Safety first

Designs consider fall heights, gaps, edges and safety zones from the first sketch. See [playground safety standards in India](/blog/playground-safety-standards-in-india.html).

## Working with clients

Every project starts with a conversation about the site and the people who will use it. We ask about the number and ages of children, the space and its shade and drainage, the budget and the look the client wants. Then we prepare a layout with safety zones and surfacing, so the client can see exactly what they will get. See [how to choose a playground equipment manufacturer](/blog/how-to-choose-a-playground-equipment-manufacturer-in-india.html) for the questions we encourage every buyer to ask, including of us.
