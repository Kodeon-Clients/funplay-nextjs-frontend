Society committees often inherit a play area that is tired: a rusty slide, a broken swing seat, paver blocks underneath. Upgrading it is one of the most visible things a committee can do, and one of the most debated. A clear process keeps it on track.

## Step 1: Ask the residents

A short survey, on paper or a messaging group, tells you:

- How many children live in the society, and their ages.
- When they play (after school, evenings, weekends).
- What families want: swings, a toddler area, an outdoor gym, seating.
- Concerns: noise near ground-floor flats, safety, lighting.

Sharing the results with residents also builds support for the budget.

## Step 2: Choose the site

## Step 3: Decide what to include

Most societies need a main play station, a separate corner for toddlers, swings, a couple of spring riders and seating. Larger societies add an outdoor gym. See [best play equipment for apartments](/blog/best-play-equipment-for-apartments-and-housing-complexes.html) for sample mixes.

## Step 4: Set a realistic budget

Include equipment, safety surfacing, civil work, installation, GST and a yearly maintenance amount. If the budget is tight, phase the project and leave space for the next phase. See [playground equipment cost in India](/blog/playground-equipment-cost-in-india.html).

## Step 5: Get approval

Prepare a short proposal with the survey results, the site, a layout and a budget range. Follow your society's bye-laws for committee and general body approval.

## Step 6: Compare suppliers

Ask at least three suppliers for a layout and a line-by-line quote for the same scope. Visit one of their society projects that is a few years old. See [how to choose a playground equipment manufacturer](/blog/how-to-choose-a-playground-equipment-manufacturer-in-india.html) and our [quote guide](/blog/quote-guide-for-playground-equipment-what-to-consider-before-purchasing.html).

## Step 7: Plan the installation

Fence off the area during work, inform residents of the dates, and agree who prepares the site. See [what happens during installation](/blog/playground-equipment-installation-process.html).

## Step 8: Agree the upkeep

Before opening, decide who checks the play area daily and monthly, where the log is kept, and whether to sign an AMC. Put the rules and a contact number on a board at the entrance.
