Two playgrounds can look identical on the day they open and very different after three monsoons. The difference is almost entirely in the materials: what the frame is made of and how it is protected, what the plastic is and how thick, and what holds it all together. This guide covers the materials used in commercial playground equipment and what to check in a specification.

## Indian conditions are hard on outdoor equipment

- **Sun:** strong UV breaks down plastics and fades colours.
- **Heat:** surfaces get hot; materials expand and contract daily.
- **Monsoon:** months of rain and humidity attack steel and timber.
- **Coastal air:** salt speeds up corrosion in Mumbai, Chennai, Goa, Kochi and other coastal cities.
- **Dust and heavy use:** wear on moving parts and surfaces.

## Steel frames

Steel is the backbone of most play equipment. How it is protected decides how long it lasts.

- **Galvanising** coats the steel in zinc, which protects it even where the surface is scratched.
- **Powder coating** adds colour and a tough outer layer.
- **Both together** (galvanised, then powder coated) give the best protection. Painted steel without galvanising starts to rust wherever the paint chips.

Ask for the pipe diameter, wall thickness and coating process. Read more in [galvanised vs powder-coated steel](/blog/galvanised-vs-powder-coated-steel-playground-equipment.html).

## Roto-moulded plastic

Slides, tunnels, roofs and many panels are made by rotational moulding: plastic powder is heated in a rotating mould to form a seamless, hollow part. Roto-moulded **linear low-density polyethylene (LLDPE)** is strong, flexible, splinter-free and safe to touch. Two things matter:

- **UV stabilisation**, so colours fade slowly and the plastic does not turn brittle.
- **Wall thickness**, which affects strength and how well the part survives knocks.

See [roto-moulded plastic vs FRP](/blog/roto-moulded-plastic-vs-frp-play-equipment.html) for how it compares with fibreglass.

## Laminates and panels

High-pressure laminate (HPL) and similar composite panels are used for decks, walls and decorative panels. They are dense, weather resistant and can carry wood-grain or bright colour finishes. Our NexLev wood-finish structures use composite laminates on galvanised frames, giving the warmth of timber without rot or termites.

## Rope and nets

Climbing nets use steel-reinforced play rope, which resists stretching, cutting and UV. Check the rope, the connectors and the tension at every monthly inspection.

## Wood

Solid timber looks beautiful in natural settings but needs the right species, pressure treatment, stainless fixings and regular care. Compare the options in [wooden vs metal vs plastic playground equipment](/blog/wooden-vs-metal-vs-plastic-playground-equipment.html).

## Fixings and moving parts

Fixings are where cheap equipment often fails first.

- **Bolts and nuts:** stainless steel or galvanised, with tamper-resistant heads and caps for public sites.
- **Bearings:** sealed bearings on merry-go-rounds, rotating climbers and gym equipment keep dust and water out.
- **Swing hangers and chains:** galvanised or stainless, with wear-resistant bushes.
