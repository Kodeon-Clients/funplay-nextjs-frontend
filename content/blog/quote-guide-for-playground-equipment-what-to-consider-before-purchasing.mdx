"How much will it cost?" is always the first question. The honest answer is that it depends on what you include, and that is exactly why two quotes for the same play area can be miles apart. This guide covers how to get an accurate quote and how to compare quotes fairly.

## What to send the supplier

The more you share up front, the more accurate the quote and the easier it is to compare suppliers.

## What a complete quote includes

A quote you can rely on lists, line by line:

1. **Each item:** model name, size, age range, user capacity and key specifications (steel, plastic, coating).
2. **Safety surfacing:** type, thickness and area in square feet or metres.
3. **Civil and base work:** excavation, levelling, concrete base, drainage, edging; or a clear note that it is in your scope.
4. **Transport** to site.
5. **Installation**, including foundations.
6. **Warranty** terms by component.
7. **GST**, shown clearly.
8. **Timeline** for manufacturing, delivery and installation.
9. **Payment terms.**

## Hidden costs to check

- **Surfacing not included,** or included only under the equipment footprint rather than the full safety zone.
- **Civil work** assumed to be "by client" without saying so.
- **Transport and unloading** charged later.
- **GST** missing from the headline figure.
- **Thinner materials** than the brochure photos suggest.
- **Customisation** charges for colours or themes.

## Comparing quotes side by side

Put every quote in one table, with the same items in the same rows. Where a supplier has left something out, write "not included" and estimate it. Only then compare the totals. It also helps to compare cost per child served rather than per item. Our guide to [playground equipment cost in India](/blog/playground-equipment-cost-in-india.html) explains what drives the price.

## Before you sign

- Approve a layout drawing with safety zones and surfacing marked.
- Get specifications, warranty and timeline in writing.
- Agree who prepares the site and by when.
- Agree the handover: final check, maintenance guide, service contact.
