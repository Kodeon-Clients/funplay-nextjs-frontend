Ask any child what they want in a playground and swings will be near the top of the list. They suit toddlers, teenagers and even grandparents, and they are one of the busiest items in every school, society and park we work in. Here are the main types and how to place them safely.

## Types of swings

### Belt or flat seat swings

The classic swing for children of about 5 and above. Flexible rubber belt seats are gentler on impact than hard seats.

### Toddler swings

Enclosed bucket seats with leg holes, so the youngest children cannot slip out. Place them in the toddler zone, ideally on their own frame.

### Nest or basket swings

A large round seat several children can share, sitting or lying down. Great for groups, and for children with limited mobility.

### Mother-and-child swings

An adult seat facing a toddler seat, so a parent can swing with a small child. Popular in societies and parks.

### Designer and themed swings

Swing frames shaped as kites, arches or characters make the swing set part of the design. Our Patang kite swing is one example.

## How much space a swing needs

A swinging seat travels well beyond the frame, in front and behind. The safety zone for a swing is therefore much longer than its footprint, and must be kept clear of other equipment, benches and paths. It must also have an impact-absorbing surface along its whole length. See [playground layout and safety zones](/blog/playground-layout-and-safety-zones.html).

## Placing swings safely

- Put swings at the edge of the play area, not in the middle.
- Keep main walking routes and slide exits away from the swing path.
- Use a low barrier, planting or a change in surface colour to guide children around.
- Separate toddler swings from big-kid swings.
- Do not mix a toddler seat and a belt seat in the same bay if older children swing high.

## Maintenance

Swing hangers, chains, shackles and seats wear faster than anything else in a playground. Check them monthly for wear at the contact points, open links and cracked seats, and replace with the manufacturer's parts.
