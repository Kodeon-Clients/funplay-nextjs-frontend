For a preschool, outdoor play is not a break from learning; it is a big part of the learning. Children aged 2 to 6 build balance, strength, coordination, language and social skills through play. The right equipment makes that easier, safer and more varied.

## What preschool children need

- **Movement:** climbing low steps, sliding, rocking, swinging, crawling, balancing.
- **Sensory play:** sand, water, textures, sounds.
- **Pretend play:** houses, shops, vehicles, anything that becomes part of a story.
- **Small-group play:** equipment two or three children can use together.
- **Safety and confidence:** equipment they can use without an adult lifting them.

Read more in [why play-based learning matters in preschool outdoor areas](/blog/why-play-based-learning-matters-in-preschool-outdoor-areas.html).

## Equipment that works well

### A low, compact play station

One small station with steps, a low platform, a short slide and play panels is the centre of most preschool play areas. Look for enclosed platforms and easy, wide steps.

### Spring riders

Cheap, small, loved by toddlers, and good for balance and core strength.

### Crawl tunnels and play houses

Children love small spaces to crawl through and hide in, and they spark pretend games.

### Play panels

Counting frames, shape boards, mazes and mirrors at child height. They can go on a boundary wall, which saves space.

### Toddler swings

Enclosed bucket seats for the youngest children, if there is room for a swing's safety zone.

### Sand and digging

Not strictly equipment, but children will spend longer here than anywhere else.

## Safety points for preschools

- Keep platforms low and enclosed.
- Avoid gaps that could trap a small head, finger or toggle on clothing.
- Choose rounded edges and smooth finishes.
- Use an impact-absorbing surface under everything. Poured rubber with built-in games works well.
- Place equipment where staff can see the whole area.
- Check the area every morning. See our [playground safety inspection checklist](/blog/playground-safety-inspection-checklist.html).

## Making the most of a small space

Most preschools in Indian cities have a courtyard, terrace or narrow strip. Use the walls for play panels, choose one compact play unit, add two spring riders and keep the middle open for running and circle games. Our [playground design ideas for small spaces](/blog/playground-design-ideas-for-small-spaces.html) has more ideas.
