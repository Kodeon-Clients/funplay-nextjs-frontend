Most schools in India want a better playground and have less money than they would like. The good news is that play value does not rise in step with price. With careful choices, a modest budget can give children a playground they use every day for years.

## Think in play per child, not price per item

A single expensive slide serves one child at a time. A compact multi play station with several slides, a climber and play panels can keep twenty children busy at once. When you compare options, ask how many children each one serves at the same time and how many different things they can do on it.

## High-value equipment for schools

### A compact multi play station

One station sized for your largest age group gives the most play per rupee. Choose a standard design and standard colours. See [what is a multi play station](/blog/what-is-a-multi-play-station.html).

### Ground-level play

Hopping posts, balancing beams, stepping stones, crawl tunnels and play panels on walls cost little, need little space and no high platforms, and work for every age. They also spread children out at break time so the main structure is less crowded.

### Classic single items

See-saws, spring riders and a swing set are cheap, durable and always used.

## Where you can save

- **Themes and custom colours.** Standard designs are cheaper and look just as good.
- **Number of items.** Fewer, better pieces beat many small ones.
- **Surfacing outside the safety zones.** Use full-thickness surfacing only under equipment; paths and open play areas can be cheaper finishes.
- **Phasing.** Install the main structure now and add swings or a toddler zone next year.

## Where you should not save

- **Safety surfacing** under the equipment. Falls on hard ground cause serious injuries. See [school playground surface materials](/blog/school-playground-surface-materials-which-one-is-best.html).
- **Materials.** Thin steel and cheap plastic fade, crack and rust within a few monsoons, and the playground has to be replaced.
- **Foundations and installation.** A wobbly structure is a dangerous structure.

<Tip title="A useful rule" text="If the budget will not stretch, reduce the number of pieces before you reduce the quality of the steel, the plastic or the surface." />

## Funding ideas

- **Parent associations and alumni** often fund a named item or zone.
- **Company CSR programmes** support projects for schools, especially in rural and government schools.
- **Annual budgets:** some schools add one item every academic year.
