Public parks are where many children in Indian cities get their only time on proper play equipment. They are also the toughest environment for it: open to everyone, all day, with little supervision. A well-planned park playground serves the whole neighbourhood for many years. A poorly planned one is broken and closed within a couple of monsoons.

## 1. Understand the users

- **Who comes, and when?** Toddlers with carers in the morning, school children in the evening, walkers and older residents early morning, families on weekends.
- **How many?** Estimate the weekend evening peak.
- **Who is missing?** Girls, teenagers and children with disabilities are often under-served.

## 3. Choose for durability

Park equipment must be commercial-grade: galvanised and powder-coated steel, UV-stabilised roto-moulded plastic, tamper-resistant fixings, sealed bearings, and foundations sized for the soil. See [top 10 commercial playground equipment options for public parks](/blog/top-10-commercial-playground-equipment-options-for-public-parks.html).

## 4. Make it inclusive

Accessible paths to and around the playground, ground-level play, nest swings, sensory panels and seating for carers. See [the growing need for inclusive playgrounds](/blog/growing-need-for-inclusive-playgrounds-in-indian-communities.html).

## 5. Surfacing and drainage

Impact-absorbing surfaces across all safety zones, on a drained base. In parks with heavy footfall, rubber is easier to keep in shape than loose fill. See [fall height and safety surfacing](/blog/playground-fall-height-and-safety-surfacing-guide.html).

## 6. Shade, seating, lighting and toilets

Trees, shade structures, benches facing the play areas, lighting for evening use, drinking water and toilets nearby decide whether families stay.

## 7. Write a clear tender

Specify performance and standards rather than a single brand, and ask for layouts, specifications and references. See [how to write a playground equipment tender specification](/blog/playground-equipment-tender-specification.html).

## 8. Budget for maintenance

Allocate a yearly maintenance budget and name the agency responsible. Without it, even good equipment deteriorates quickly in a public park. See our [playground safety inspection checklist](/blog/playground-safety-inspection-checklist.html).
