A theme turns a set of equipment into a place: a castle to defend, a jungle to explore, a robot to climb. Customisation can be as simple as a colour change or as ambitious as a one-off design. Here are the options and what each involves.

## Choosing a theme

- **Site:** a jungle theme in a green park, a castle near heritage architecture, a ship by the sea.
- **Users:** younger children love characters and stories; older children prefer challenge and bold forms.
- **Brand:** resorts and builders may want a theme linked to their identity.
- **Longevity:** a theme should still feel right in ten years.

## Fully custom designs

When a project needs something unique, our design team can develop a custom structure. Expect design development, approvals and possibly new moulds. See how we work in [FunPlay's design process](/blog/unleashing-imaginative-play-funplays-design-process-unveiled.html).
