Most playground buyers never see where their equipment is made. We think they should, because what happens in the factory decides how the equipment looks and performs years later. Here is how a playground is made at our facility in Vasai, near Mumbai.

## From drawing to production plan

Every order starts as drawings: the site layout, then CAD models of each structure. Once the layout is approved, the production team plans each part: what is moulded, what is fabricated, which colours, and in what sequence, so the whole order comes together on time.

## Roto-moulding the plastic parts

Slides, tunnels, roofs and many panels are made by rotational moulding. Plastic powder with UV stabilisers goes into a metal mould, which is heated while it turns on two axes. The plastic coats the inside of the mould evenly and forms a seamless, hollow part with rounded edges and no joints to crack. Read more in [roto-moulded plastic vs FRP](/blog/roto-moulded-plastic-vs-frp-play-equipment.html).

## Fabricating the steel frames

Posts, beams and frames are cut, bent and welded from steel pipe. All cutting, drilling and welding is done before finishing, so no bare steel is left exposed. Frames are galvanised and powder coated in the chosen colour. See [galvanised vs powder-coated steel](/blog/galvanised-vs-powder-coated-steel-playground-equipment.html).

## Panels, decks and rope

Composite laminate panels and decks are cut and edged. Climbing nets use steel-reinforced play rope with proper connectors.

## Checks before dispatch

Parts are checked for finish, dimensions, sharp edges and correct fixings. Structures are trial-fitted where needed so installation on site goes smoothly.

## Packing and installation

Parts are packed to protect finishes in transit and shipped across India. Our own installation teams set the foundations, assemble the structures and lay or coordinate the surfacing. See [what happens during installation](/blog/playground-equipment-installation-process.html).

## Why making it ourselves matters

Because design, moulding and fabrication happen under one roof, we can change colours, adapt a design to a site and supply spare parts years later. If you would like to see it for yourself, ask our team for a factory visit.
