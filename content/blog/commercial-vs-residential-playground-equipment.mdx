Search for "playground equipment" online and you will find two very different kinds of product side by side: home play sets for a garden, and commercial equipment for schools, societies and parks. They can look alike in a photo. They are not the same, and for any shared space the difference matters.

## What commercial equipment means

**Commercial playground equipment** is designed for public and shared use: schools, housing societies, parks, resorts, hospitals, malls and campuses. That means:

- Many children a day, every day, for years.
- Children of different sizes and ages using the same equipment.
- Supervision that is occasional rather than constant.
- Full exposure to sun, rain, dust and in some places salt air.
- Occasional misuse: teenagers on toddler swings, adults sitting on spring riders.

**Residential or home equipment** is designed for a family garden: a few children, close supervision, lighter use.

## Who needs commercial equipment?

- **Schools and preschools.** Heavy, concentrated use at break times.
- **Housing societies.** Shared by every family, used in the evenings and on weekends with irregular supervision. The society is responsible for the safety of residents' children.
- **Builders and developers.** The play area is part of what buyers are promised and must still look good years after handover. See [playground amenities for real estate projects](/blog/playground-amenities-for-real-estate-projects.html).
- **Parks and municipal bodies.** The toughest use of all, open to everyone.
- **Resorts, hotels and clubs.** Guests expect equipment that looks well kept.

Even a small society play area with only three pieces needs commercial equipment. Size is not the issue; shared use is.

## How to tell from a quote

A quote for commercial equipment should state:

1. The **standard** the equipment is designed to.
2. **Material specifications**: steel diameter and thickness, coating, plastic type.
3. **Fall height and age range** of each unit.
4. **Foundation details** and who installs.
5. **Warranty terms** by component.
6. **Recommended surfacing** for each unit.

If a quote only shows a picture and a price, ask for these details before comparing it with others. Our [quote guide](/blog/quote-guide-for-playground-equipment-what-to-consider-before-purchasing.html) has the full list.
