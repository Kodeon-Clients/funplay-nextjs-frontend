Cities in India are growing fast. Every day, new buildings, roads, and shopping malls are being built. But while we build more for adults, we often forget about children. Many Indian cities do not have enough safe and clean playgrounds. This is a big issue that needs attention.

<h2 className={"h3"}>What Are the Main Challenges?</h2>

1. **Lack of Space**<br /> Cities are crowded. Open land is rare and expensive. Empty areas are often used for buildings or parking, not for parks or playgrounds. In many neighborhoods, children play on the streets or in unsafe places.
2. **Poor Maintenance**<br /> Some cities do have playgrounds, but many of them are broken, dirty, or full of garbage. Swings are damaged, slides are rusty, and there is no safety flooring. Without regular cleaning and repair, these spaces become dangerous.
3. **Safety Concerns**<br /> Many parks are near busy roads or not fenced properly. Some areas are not well-lit or feel unsafe, especially for girls and younger children. Parents are often afraid to let their children play there alone.
4. **Lack of Inclusive Design**<br /> Most playgrounds are not built for all children. Children with disabilities usually cannot use the equipment. There are no ramps, special swings, or sensory play options for them.
5. **Not a Priority**<br /> City planning often focuses on transportation, housing, and business. Playgrounds are seen as “less important,” so they don’t get enough funds or attention. Some rules say new buildings must leave space for parks, but these rules are often not followed.

### What Needs to Be Done?

- ✅ **Protect and Use Available Land**<br /> Cities should protect open spaces and plan properly. Every area should have a park within walking distance. Government rules must be followed strictly to save space for public use.
- ✅ **Involve Local Communities**<br /> Residents, schools, and local leaders should work together. Communities can help clean, watch over, and take care of playgrounds.
- ✅ **Build Inclusive and Low-Cost Playgrounds**<br /> Use smart designs that include all children, including those with disabilities. Materials like tires, wood, and ropes can create fun and safe play spaces at low cost. Organizations like Anthill Creations have already done this in many places.
- ✅ **Focus on Safety and Cleanliness**<br /> Install fences, soft ground, proper lighting, and dustbins. Clean the area regularly and check equipment for repairs.

By addressing these challenges, Indian cities can create playgrounds that are safe, inclusive, and enjoyable for all children. It is essential to act now to ensure a better future for our children.
