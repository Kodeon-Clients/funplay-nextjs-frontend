<h2 className={"h3"}>Why Surface Choice Matters in Indian Schools</h2>

- **Safety:** Prevents injuries from falls
- **Drainage:** Helps avoid water logging during monsoons
- **Durability:** Withstands daily wear from heavy usage
- **Heat Management:** Avoids burns in hot weather
- **Compliance:** Required for BIS and international safety standards

### 1. Rubber Tiles

**Best for:** Private schools, urban campuses, preschools looking to **install premium safety flooring**.

<ul><li><strong>Pros:</strong></li> <ul><li>Excellent fall protection</li> <li>Available in bright, child-friendly colours</li> <li>Easy to clean and maintain</li> <li>Shock absorbent and weather resistant</li> </ul> <li><strong>Cons:</strong></li> <ul><li>Higher upfront cost</li> <li>Needs flat concrete base for installation</li> </ul></ul>

### 2. Artificial Grass (Turf)

**Best for:** Premium schools, residential societies with shared playgrounds **seeking lush, low-maintenance options**.

<ul><li><strong>Pros:</strong></li> <ul><li>Looks lush year-round</li> <li>Minimal dust or mud</li> <li>Soft and safe for toddlers</li> <li>UV stabilized: doesn’t fade easily</li> </ul> <li><strong>Cons:</strong></li> <ul><li>Requires periodic brushing</li> <li>Slight heat retention in summers</li> </ul></ul>

*Need professional turf installation?* **[Contact our experts today.](/contact-us.html)**

### 3. Sand

**Best for:** Budget-conscious schools, rural campuses **exploring affordable surface materials**.

<ul><li><strong>Pros:</strong></li> <ul><li>Inexpensive</li> <li>Natural and soft</li> <li>Great for free play and digging</li> </ul> <li><strong>Cons:</strong></li> <ul><li>Can attract stray animals if not fenced</li> <li>Needs frequent raking and cleaning</li> <li>Poor drainage in heavy rains</li> </ul></ul>

*Want to know how much sand setup costs?* **[See our budget packages.](/contact-us.html)**

*Tip:* Use coarse river sand, not construction sand.

### 4. Mulch or Wood Chips

**Best for:** Nature-integrated schools, eco campuses **looking for eco-friendly playground surfacing**.

<ul><li><strong>Pros:</strong></li> <ul><li>Eco-friendly</li> <li>Absorbs impact from falls</li> <li>Aesthetically pleasing in natural settings</li> </ul> <li><strong>Cons:</strong></li> <ul><li>Needs replenishing every 6–12 months</li> <li>Attracts insects if not treated</li> </ul></ul>

*Get a price estimate for wood chip surfacing.* **[Talk to our sales team.](/contact-us.html)**

### 5. Interlocking EVA Foam Mats

**Best for:** Indoor play zones, kindergarten classrooms **requiring safe indoor flooring**.

<ul><li><strong>Pros:</strong></li> <ul><li>Easy to assemble and move</li> <li>Great for floor games</li> <li>Cushioned and soft</li> </ul> <li><strong>Cons:</strong></li> <ul><li>Not suitable for outdoor installation</li> <li>Can become slippery if wet</li> </ul></ul>

### Playground Surface + Equipment = Full Safety Setup

- **High slides or climbers →** Rubber tile or mulch
- **Sandpits or swings →** Sand or turf
- **Interactive play →** Foam or rubber-based flooring

*FunPlay Tip:* Combine surfaces for a cost-effective hybrid layout.
