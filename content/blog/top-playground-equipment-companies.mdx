When people search for "playground equipment companies near me", they are usually looking for two things at once: someone who can make good equipment, and someone who will turn up when it needs attention. Those are not always the same company. This guide helps you work out what you actually need nearby, and how to shortlist and verify a supplier.

## What "near me" really matters for

- **Site visits** before the layout is finalised.
- **Installation**, which takes days or weeks on site.
- **After-sales service**: inspections, repairs and spares over the years.

It matters much less for **manufacturing**. Playground equipment travels well; we regularly install equipment made in Vasai, near Mumbai, at sites from Gujarat to Tamil Nadu. What you need is a supplier that can be on your site when it counts.

## How to build a shortlist

1. **Search** for playground equipment manufacturers or suppliers in your city and state.
2. **Ask around.** Schools, societies and architects nearby can tell you who installed their play areas and how the service has been.
3. **Check project lists.** A supplier's website should show real projects with names and locations.
4. **Shortlist three** that make or supply the type of equipment you need.

## Red flags

- No real project names, only stock photos.
- Prices quoted without asking about your site, users or surface.
- Certifications claimed but no documents shown when asked.
- No written warranty, or a warranty with no detail.
- Pressure to pay in full before the layout is agreed.
