Four months of rain, humidity and storms do more damage to outdoor equipment than the rest of the year together. A simple plan, split into before, during and after the monsoon, keeps the play area safe and adds years to its life.

## Before the monsoon (May to early June)

<Checklist title="Pre-monsoon checklist" items={["Clear drains, gullies and channels around the play area","Check that rubber and turf surfaces slope to the drains; mark low spots","Tighten all reachable bolts and replace missing bolt caps","Clean and touch up chipped paint or powder coat, especially at the base of posts","Lubricate moving parts as the supplier recommends; check bearings on merry-go-rounds and gym machines","Trim overhanging branches that could fall in a storm","Secure loose items: bins, temporary shade, signs","Top up sand or loose fill where it has scattered"]} />

## During the monsoon

- **After every heavy spell,** walk the play area: standing water, mud, fallen branches, debris.
- **Close equipment** while surfaces are wet and slippery. A simple sign helps.
- **Watch the drains.** Blocked drains cause water to pool and lift rubber edges.
- **Check swings and chains** after strong winds; they can wrap around the top bar.
- **Keep an eye on algae and moss** on shaded surfaces; scrub them before they get slippery.

## After the monsoon (October)

Do a full inspection, ideally with your supplier:

- **Rust:** base of posts, welds, fixings. Clean and touch up surface rust; report anything deeper.
- **Foundations:** soaked soil can loosen footings. Shake every post.
- **Surfacing:** lifted tiles, cracks or sinking in poured rubber, compacted or washed-away sand.
- **Moving parts:** bearings, hangers and chains that sound rough or show wear.
- **Wood and laminate:** swelling, splits, delamination.
- **Electrical:** lighting near the play area.

## Coastal sites

Near the sea, salt in the air speeds up corrosion:

- Rinse equipment with fresh water after the monsoon and every few weeks in the dry season.
- Inspect the base of posts and fixings every month.
- Touch up coatings promptly.
- Ask your supplier about stainless fixings when you buy.

<Tip title="Plan the work" text="Book your annual inspection or AMC visit for late May and again in October. That catches pre-monsoon issues and post-monsoon damage at the right time." />
