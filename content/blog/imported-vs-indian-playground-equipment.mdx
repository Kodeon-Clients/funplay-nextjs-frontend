For larger projects, especially premium townships, international schools and resorts, the question often comes up: should we import playground equipment or buy from an Indian manufacturer? There is no single right answer. Here is an honest comparison.

## Quality is about specification, not the country

Good and poor equipment is made everywhere. What makes equipment last is:

- the steel, its thickness and its coating,
- the grade and wall thickness of the plastic,
- the fixings and moving parts,
- the safety standard it is designed to, and
- how it is installed and maintained.

Several Indian manufacturers, including FunPlay, design to the European standard EN 1176 that most imported brands follow. Compare suppliers on these points rather than on where the factory is.

## When imported equipment makes sense

- An architect has specified a particular international product.
- The project needs a specific item no Indian manufacturer makes.
- The budget and timeline allow for longer lead times and higher spares costs.

If you import, confirm who stocks spares in India, who installs, and how quickly they respond.

## When Indian equipment makes sense

- You want the layout and colours adapted to your site.
- The timeline is tight.
- You want spares and service from the manufacturer for years.
- The budget needs to go further: more play for the same money.
